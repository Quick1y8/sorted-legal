Sorted — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026 · Developer: TaraPDF · Contact: kaanyakuphancabukoglu@gmail.com

Sorted is a puzzle game. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. We keep it to the minimum the game needs.

What we do not collect

  • No account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • Your progress (levels, stars, settings, collection) is stored only on your device.

Data used by services in the app

Game analytics (Google Firebase Analytics). We count game events such as starting or finishing a level, number of moves, hints used and ads shown, and which starting scene a new player saw (for a design test). Firebase also records basic device information (model, OS version, language, approximate country from IP address) and an app instance ID. We use this to see which levels are too hard and whether players come back, not to identify you.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). If the app crashes, a report with the error, device model and OS version is sent so we can fix it.

Remote settings (Firebase Remote Config). The app downloads a few settings (for example, the design test above) so we can adjust them without an update.

Ads (Google AdMob). The free version shows ads: none in the first 5 levels, then at most one every 3 levels, never during a level, plus optional ads you choose to watch. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and information about ad interactions to show and measure ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first and can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices. On iOS, ads are personalized only if you allow tracking. The one-time Supporter purchase removes all ads.

Daily reminder (optional). If you turn on the daily reminder, the notification is scheduled on your device only. No data is sent for it, and you can turn it off in Settings.

Purchases (Google Play / Apple App Store). Purchases are handled by the store. We only receive confirmation of what was bought; we never see your payment details.

Children

Sorted is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Your choices

  • Ad consent: Settings → Privacy & ad choices (where available).
  • Advertising ID: reset or limit it in your device settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all game data stored on your device.
  • Analytics data is kept by Google for up to 14 months. To ask about or delete data linked to your device, email us.
